Course Description

Learn to live from your heavenly identity through faith, wisdom, spiritual discernment, and the power of the Holy Spirit as a normal way of life. This capstone course integrates the module's core themes, renewed thinking, sonship, authority, and rest, into a consistent, sustainable supernatural lifestyle. Students learn to walk in the miraculous as an overflow of relationship with God rather than performance, finishing the program equipped to live and minister from continual dependence on the Spirit.
