NT 405 — The Book of Revelation

Christ's Ultimate Victory

Module IV: New Testament Survey · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

Course Description

Understand Christ's ultimate victory, God's final redemptive plan, the new creation, and the believer's eternal hope through the book of Revelation. This course walks through John's apocalyptic vision, including the letters to the seven churches, the unfolding judgments, the defeat of evil, and the glorious return of Christ to establish His eternal Kingdom. Students are equipped to interpret apocalyptic literature responsibly and to live with confident hope.
