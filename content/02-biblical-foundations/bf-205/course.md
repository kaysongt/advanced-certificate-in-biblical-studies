# BF 205 — Christ-Centered Interpretation

### Discovering Jesus in Every Book

**Module II: Biblical Foundations** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

See how every book of the Bible, from Genesis to Revelation, ultimately points to Jesus Christ as its central figure and fulfillment. This course explores the biblical patterns, promises, types, prophecies, and foreshadowings that reveal Christ throughout both testaments, training students to recognize Him even in the Old Testament's law, history, and poetry. This Christ-centered approach to interpretation deepens biblical understanding and cultivates a richer, more worshipful appreciation for the Savior at the heart of Scripture.
