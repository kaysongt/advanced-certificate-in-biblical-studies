### A Journey Through the Bible

**Module II: Biblical Foundations** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Discover the origin, structure, purpose, and authority of the Bible as the inspired, inerrant Word of God. This course surveys how the sixty-six books were written, compiled, and preserved across centuries, while introducing the major divisions of Scripture and their unique contributions to God's overarching story. Students gain a solid foundation for understanding the Bible as one unified, life-transforming narrative, equipping them with the framework needed to navigate Scripture accurately throughout the rest of their studies.
