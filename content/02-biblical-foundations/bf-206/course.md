# BF 206 — Biblical Interpretation Errors

### Common Errors in Bible Interpretation

**Module II: Biblical Foundations** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Identify and avoid the most common mistakes that lead to misunderstanding and misapplication of Scripture, including proof-texting, allegorizing, and ignoring historical and literary context. This course equips students to recognize subtle interpretive pitfalls that produce doctrinal error and unhealthy teaching, while modeling how to handle God's Word with integrity and care. Through guided practice and case studies, students develop sound, biblically faithful habits of study that protect both their own theology and those they teach.
