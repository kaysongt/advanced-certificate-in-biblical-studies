# BF 202 — Bible Study & Exegesis

### How to Effectively Study the Bible

**Module II: Biblical Foundations** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Learn practical, time-tested methods for studying the Bible individually, applying observation, interpretation, and application in a simple, repeatable process. This course equips students with tools such as inductive study, topical study, and word study methods, empowering them to dig into Scripture with confidence rather than depending solely on others' teaching. Students develop lifelong habits of personal Bible study that deepen their walk with God and prepare them to guide others in studying Scripture for themselves.
