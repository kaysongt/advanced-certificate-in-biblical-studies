# Module II — Certificate in Biblical Foundations

**Unlocked Series** · 36 Hours · 6 Courses

## Module Overview

Build a strong foundation for understanding the Bible as one unified revelation of God's redemptive plan. This module equips students with the essential tools to understand Scripture accurately, interpret it faithfully, and recognize Jesus Christ as its central theme from Genesis to Revelation.

## What Students Gain

Learn to read, interpret, and study the Bible accurately for yourself, tracing God's redemptive story from Genesis to Revelation and recognizing Christ throughout every book.
