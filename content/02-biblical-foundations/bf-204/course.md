# BF 204 — Redemptive Theology

### The Story of Redemption

**Module II: Biblical Foundations** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Trace the unfolding story of God's redemptive plan as it progresses from Genesis to Revelation in one unbroken narrative. This course reveals the unity of Scripture by following the development of God's kingdom, covenants, promises, and salvation across every era of biblical history, from creation and the fall to Israel, exile, and the Church. Students learn to read individual passages within this larger redemptive framework, deepening their appreciation for how the whole Bible tells one grand story.
