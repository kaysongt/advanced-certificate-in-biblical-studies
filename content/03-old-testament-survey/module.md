# Module III — Certificate in Old Testament Survey

**Exploring the Foundations of Redemption** · 30 Hours · 5 Courses

## Module Overview

Journey through the Old Testament book by book to understand God's unfolding covenant relationship with humanity. This module reveals the historical, prophetic, poetic, and theological foundations that prepare the way for Christ and the fulfillment of God's redemptive purposes.

## What Students Gain

Understand the full sweep of God's covenant story, book by book, from creation through the Law, history, wisdom, and prophets, and how it prepares the way for Christ.
