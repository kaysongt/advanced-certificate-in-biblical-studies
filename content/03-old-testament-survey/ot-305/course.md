# OT 305 — The Minor Prophets

### Hosea – Malachi

**Module III: Old Testament Survey** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Examine the twelve Minor Prophets and God's consistent call to repentance, covenant faithfulness, and future restoration. Though brief, these books carry powerful messages of justice, mercy, and hope for both Israel and the surrounding nations. Students trace common themes across the twelve, including the Day of the Lord, God's compassion toward the repentant, and the promised restoration that anticipates the coming Messiah and His eternal Kingdom, learning to apply their urgent calls to holiness in a modern context.
