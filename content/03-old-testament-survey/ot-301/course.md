**Module III: Old Testament Survey** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

## Course Description

Study the foundations of creation, covenant, law, and redemption established in the five books of Moses. This course traces humanity's fall, God's covenant with Abraham, Israel's deliverance from Egypt, the giving of the Law at Sinai, and the nation's preparation to enter the Promised Land. Students gain a foundational understanding of God's holiness, covenant faithfulness, and redemptive purposes that shape the theological framework for the rest of Scripture.
