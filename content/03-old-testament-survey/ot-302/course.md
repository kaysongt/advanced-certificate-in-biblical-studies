# OT 302 — The Historical Books

### Joshua – Esther

**Module III: Old Testament Survey** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Follow Israel's journey from conquest to kingdom to exile and restoration across the historical narrative books. This course examines the rise and fall of Israel's monarchy, the ministries of key judges and kings, and God's providential faithfulness even amid the nation's repeated failure and idolatry. Students learn how these historical narratives reveal God's patience, justice, and covenant loyalty throughout the unfolding story of His people, and how to apply these lessons to seasons of both national and personal history today.
