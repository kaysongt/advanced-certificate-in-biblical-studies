# Module IV — Certificate in New Testament Survey

**The Fulfillment of God's Redemptive Plan** · 30 Hours · 5 Courses

## Module Overview

Explore the New Testament from the life and ministry of Jesus to the birth of the Church and the consummation of God's Kingdom. This module provides a comprehensive understanding of each New Testament book, highlighting its historical context, theological message, and practical application for believers today.

## What Students Gain

Grasp the life and ministry of Jesus, the birth of the Church, and the doctrine and practice handed down through the apostles, equipping you to teach the New Testament with confidence.
