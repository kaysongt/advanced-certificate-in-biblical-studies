# NT 401 — The Four Gospels

### Matthew – John

**Module IV: New Testament Survey** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Explore the life, teachings, miracles, death, resurrection, and mission of Jesus Christ through the four Gospel accounts. This course examines each Gospel's unique audience, purpose, and portrait of Christ, from Matthew's presentation of Jesus as King to John's emphasis on His divine nature. Students gain a comprehensive, harmonized understanding of Christ's earthly ministry, equipping them to teach the Gospels accurately and grow in devotion to the Savior they proclaim.
