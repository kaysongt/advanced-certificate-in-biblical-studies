# NT 402 — Acts & the Pauline Epistles

**Module IV: New Testament Survey** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Discover the growth of the early Church, the spread of the Gospel, and the foundational doctrines of grace through Acts and Paul's early letters. This course traces the apostles' Spirit-empowered mission from Jerusalem to Rome alongside Paul's core teaching on justification, sanctification, church order, and the return of Christ. Students develop a clear grasp of apostolic doctrine and the practical outworking of grace within the early Christian community.
