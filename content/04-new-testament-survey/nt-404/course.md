# NT 404 — The General Epistles

### Hebrews – Jude

**Module IV: New Testament Survey** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Study practical instruction on faith, endurance, holiness, and defending biblical truth through the General Epistles. This course explores the superiority of Christ, the necessity of living faith expressed through works, perseverance amid suffering, and warnings against false teaching that threatened the early Church's health and doctrine. Students grow in discernment and spiritual maturity, learning to apply these urgent, practical letters to contemporary challenges of faith, holiness, and Christian community life.
