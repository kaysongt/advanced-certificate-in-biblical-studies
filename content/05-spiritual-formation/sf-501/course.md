# SF 501 — Not Conformed

### The Power of a Renewed Mind

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Learn how renewing your mind through God's Word transforms your thinking, decisions, and daily life, breaking old patterns shaped by the world. This course examines the biblical process of mental and spiritual transformation, teaching students to identify unbiblical thought patterns and replace them with truth. Students discover practical tools for sustained renewal, including Scripture meditation and prayer, and understand why a renewed mind is essential to living a surrendered, Spirit-led life that reflects Christ rather than culture.
