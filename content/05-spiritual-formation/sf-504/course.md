# SF 504 — Powered

### The Dynamics of Dunamis

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Understand how the Holy Spirit empowers believers for holy living, effective ministry, and supernatural impact through the biblical concept of dunamis, God's miraculous power. This course examines the Spirit's enabling presence for signs, wonders, and everyday faithfulness, teaching students to move beyond information into Spirit-empowered action. Students learn to recognize and steward this power responsibly, growing in boldness and effectiveness as they minister to others and represent Christ's Kingdom with genuine supernatural authority and grace.
