# SF 503 — Uiothesia

### The Adoption of Sons

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Discover your identity as a mature son or daughter of God and learn to live confidently from your inheritance in Christ. This course explores the biblical concept of adoption, examining the rights, privileges, and responsibilities that come with being fully welcomed into God's family. Students move beyond an orphan mindset of fear and striving into a secure identity rooted in sonship, learning to walk in the confidence, authority, and intimacy that flow naturally from knowing who they are in Christ.
