# SF 502 — Finding Truth

### Living from Reality as God Sees It

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Develop a thoroughly biblical worldview by learning to see reality through God's truth rather than cultural deception or personal feeling. This course trains students to filter every belief, value, and decision through the lens of Scripture, building discernment for navigating a world full of competing truth claims. Students grow in confidence to stand firm on biblical conviction, recognize deception, and live from a settled, unshakable foundation that shapes every area of thought and daily practice.
