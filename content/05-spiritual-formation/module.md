# Module V — Certificate in Spiritual Formation

**Essentials of Spiritual Growth** · 40 Hours · 10 Courses

## Module Overview

Move beyond biblical knowledge to personal transformation. This module develops Christlike character, spiritual maturity, Kingdom identity, and a supernatural lifestyle by equipping students to think biblically, walk in the Spirit, live from their new creation identity, and fulfill God's purpose with wisdom, power, and unwavering faith.

## What Students Gain

Move beyond information into transformation: a renewed mind, a secure identity, and a Spirit-empowered, supernatural lifestyle you can sustain for the long term.
