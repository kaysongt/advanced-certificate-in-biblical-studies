# SF 509 — Built Differently

### Faith Lifestyle

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Understand the basic principles of the God-kind of faith and how it operates differently from natural, human reasoning. This course examines faith as a spiritual force, exploring how believers can develop and exercise faith the way God does to overcome obstacles and walk in victory. Students grow in their identity as a new creation in Christ, learning practical steps to build unwavering faith that produces tangible results in every area of life, family, and ministry.
