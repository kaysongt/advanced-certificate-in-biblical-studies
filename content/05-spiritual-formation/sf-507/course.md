# SF 507 — Rest Mode

### The End of Spiritual Hustle

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Experience freedom from striving by learning to live from Christ's finished work and the grace of God rather than constant spiritual effort. This course addresses the exhaustion of performance-based faith, teaching students the biblical difference between resting in grace and working from grace. Students discover practical rhythms of Sabbath, trust, and surrender that replace anxious hustle with peace, learning to minister and serve from a place of restored identity and abundance rather than fear or self-effort.
