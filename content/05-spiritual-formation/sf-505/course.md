# SF 505 — Authorized

### The Dynamics of Exousia

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Learn how to exercise the spiritual authority Christ has given every believer with humility, wisdom, and confidence through the biblical concept of exousia, delegated authority. This course examines the believer's position in Christ and the practical implications of that authority over sin, sickness, and spiritual opposition. Students explore how authority differs from power and why both matter for effective ministry, growing in the confidence to walk out their calling boldly while remaining rooted in humility.
