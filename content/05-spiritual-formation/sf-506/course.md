# SF 506 — Life On Point

### Purposeful Living

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Clarify your calling and align your gifts, passions, and purpose with God's divine assignment for your life through intentional, purposeful living. This course helps students identify how their unique design, experiences, and spiritual gifting fit within God's larger Kingdom purposes, moving them from uncertainty into clarity and direction. Students leave with practical tools for discerning calling, setting Kingdom-focused priorities, and pursuing their God-given purpose with confidence, ensuring daily choices align with eternal significance and impact.
