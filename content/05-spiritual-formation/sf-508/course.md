# SF 508 — Prayer, Not Religion

### Prayer Lifestyle

**Module V: Spiritual Formation** · 4 Hours · 4 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Develop a vibrant prayer life rooted in genuine relationship, intimacy, faith, and ongoing fellowship with God rather than religious routine or empty ritual. This course distinguishes authentic, Spirit-led prayer from performative religious activity, teaching students how to approach God with confidence, honesty, and expectancy. Students explore various biblical models and postures of prayer, including intercession, worship, and listening prayer, developing a sustainable, joy-filled communication with God that flows from relationship rather than obligation or fear.
