*What We Believe: God*

**Module I: Systematic Theology** · 6 Hours · 6 Lessons

---

## Course Description

Explore the biblical revelation of God: His nature, attributes, sovereignty, and works. Students examine His holiness, love, omniscience, omnipotence, and omnipresence as revealed throughout Scripture, along with His eternal purposes and unfailing covenant faithfulness toward His people. This course establishes a thoroughly God-centered worldview, teaching believers to approach every area of life, relationship, and ministry from a settled confidence in who God is, equipped to worship rightly, trust deeply, and represent the Creator's character with conviction.
