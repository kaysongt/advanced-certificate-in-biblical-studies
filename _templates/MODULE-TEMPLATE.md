# Module I — Certificate in Systematic Theology

**What We Believe Series** · 36 Hours · 6 Courses

## Module Overview

Build a solid theological foundation by exploring the essential doctrines of the Christian faith. This module equips students with a biblical understanding of God, salvation, the Church, eternity, and other foundational truths, enabling them to think theologically, live biblically, and defend the faith with confidence.

## What Students Gain

Gain a confident, biblically grounded understanding of core Christian doctrine, from the nature of God to the believer's eternal hope, that you can explain, teach, and defend with clarity.
