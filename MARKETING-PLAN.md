# Advanced Certificate Announcement Plan

This is the internal launch plan for the Advanced Certificate in Biblical Studies. Complete the announcement gate before promoting the training publicly.

## Announcement Gate

- Module I is fully available, including lessons, textbooks, assessments, and the 80% pass rule.
- The completion window for each self-paced certificate is approved and written into the enrollment terms.
- Manual invoicing or checkout is tested from enrollment through access activation.
- Opening and closing module video briefings are uploaded or assigned an owner and delivery date.
- Community group moderation is assigned to named KingsWord team members.
- Community participation and extra-credit criteria are approved in writing; participation must not change the required 80% assessment pass mark without a published rubric.
- The assessment folder, accessibility pass, mobile QA, and contact routes are checked.
- The launch message, email sequence, visuals, and registration owner are approved.

## Positioning

Core promise: a structured, Bible-centered path for believers who want to understand Scripture, explain doctrine with confidence, and grow in practical Christian living.

Supporting points:

- 172 contact hours across five certificate programs.
- Begin with the available Systematic Theology certificate or reserve a place in the full program.
- Self-paced study within a defined completion window.
- Structured lessons, customized textbooks, topic quizzes, and course assessments.
- Enrolled community groups, instructor feedback, and opening/closing video briefings.
- A clear 80% pass mark for every topic quiz and course assessment.

## Audience

- Pastors, ministry leaders, Bible teachers, and church workers.
- Small-group leaders, Bible-school students, missionaries, and new believers seeking a sound foundation.
- Existing KingsWord church networks and ministry partners.

## Six-Week Rollout

### Six Weeks Before Announcement

- Finalize the offer, completion-window policy, payment route, and enrollment responses.
- Record or schedule Module I opening and closing videos.
- Assign at least one community moderator and create a short discussion and escalation policy.
- Build a landing-page message bank: program overview, tuition, assessment standard, and release schedule.

### Four Weeks Before Announcement

- Prepare the announcement video, a one-page overview, social graphics, and a short course-preview clip.
- Draft a five-email sequence: announcement, curriculum overview, instructor/story, assessment/community explanation, and final invitation.
- Create referral copy for campus pastors, ministry leaders, and alumni.
- Set up source tracking for email, church partners, social posts, and direct referrals.

### Two Weeks Before Announcement

- Run an internal enrollment dry run from account creation to active course access.
- Test Module I on mobile, desktop, and slow connections.
- Seed the first community prompts and publish moderator availability.
- Brief every leader who will answer questions so the tuition, pass mark, completion window, and release schedule are stated consistently.

### Announcement Week

- Publish the landing page and announcement video together.
- Send the primary email to existing contacts and distribute the partner toolkit.
- Post short curriculum highlights over several days instead of repeating one generic announcement.
- Hold a daily review of inquiries, enrollment friction, and community moderation needs.

### First Four Weeks After Launch

- Share student questions, course moments, and instructor insights with permission.
- Follow up with people who started but did not complete enrollment.
- Review community activity weekly and award any approved extra credit using the published rubric.
- Report conversion, enrollment, active-study, assessment, and engagement metrics to the launch team.

## Minimum Asset Set

- Program landing page with Module I availability and future release months.
- A 60 to 90 second announcement video from Dr. Kay.
- Module I opening video and closing video.
- Tuition and assessment explainer.
- Community-group and extra-credit explainer.
- Partner email copy, social captions, and mobile-ready graphics.
- Enrollment support response templates.

## Metrics

- Landing-page visits by source.
- Enrollment starts, completed registrations, invoices sent, and activated enrollments.
- Module I starts, topic completion, and assessment pass rate.
- Community participation: contributors, posts, moderator response time, and approved extra-credit awards.
- Questions or support issues that indicate unclear copy or a blocked enrollment path.

## Launch Owner Checklist

- Program and academic policy owner: approves completion window and extra-credit rubric.
- Community owner: moderates groups and escalates student concerns.
- Content owner: confirms videos, assessment links, and course availability.
- Marketing owner: owns the campaign calendar, partner distribution, and reporting.
- Technical owner: validates enrollment, access, links, and release-date messaging.
